Portillo Run Labeller

Twenty-four recorded descents over three days, grouped by the lift that carried them up. Pick two off the same lift and the map draws them together, so “is this the same piste?” becomes something you look at rather than remember. The number under the map is what the shape metric scores that pair — your answer is the one that decides whether it means anything.
